🛡️ NetMuzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026
Author & Copyright Owner: Marcin Stankiewicz

Summary & Open Source Transparency: NetMuzzle is an open-source, free software project licensed under the MIT License. The entire source code is publicly auditable on GitHub. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or network traffic.

1. Overview and Open Source Philosophy

NetMuzzle ("the App") was developed by Marcin Stankiewicz. To demonstrate complete transparency and prove that no hidden telemetry or backdoor tracking exists, the project is 100% open source.

2. Pricing and Future Advertising Disclosure

NetMuzzle is provided as a free application. In the current version, the App is completely ad-free. In future releases, non-intrusive advertisements may be introduced to support ongoing maintenance and development. Any future advertising integration will comply strictly with Google Play policies and will never compromise the local firewall blackhole or inspect your network activity.

3. Data Collection and Transmission

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, device IDs, or IP addresses.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The App does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or crash-reporting services.
  • No External Servers: The App does not communicate with external servers. All operations happen entirely offline on your device.

4. Use of VpnService

NetMuzzle uses Android's system VpnService API strictly as a local network blackhole (sink):

  • Only applications explicitly placed on the user's blocklist are routed into the local VPN interface.
  • Packets sent into the VPN interface are discarded immediately on the device.
  • We do not inspect, log, analyze, or forward any network traffic or DNS queries.
  • Unblocked applications bypass the VPN interface entirely at the Linux kernel level.

5. Use of QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Permission

The App requests the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission solely to display the list of installed applications on your device, allowing you to select which applications you wish to mute/block. This list never leaves your device.

6. Data Storage

Your preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted Jetpack DataStore. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data.

7. Contact and Source Code

Source code repository and issue tracker: https://github.com/mastai-dev/NetMuzzle.
